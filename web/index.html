        For analysts

        Trade-off space: what do you give up when steering?

        Every combination of the four controls on the RQ1 grid (192 settings), for this user and for 2,000 test users. Settings on the frontier get the most relevance for how far they steer; the rest give up relevance for nothing. Click any dot to load that setting.

        Similarity map: is the restaurant space shaped by cuisine or by place?

        Every restaurant with enough reviews before the snapshot, placed by who reviewed it, so places with overlapping reviewers sit together. This user's candidates are on top. Use it to ask where a list comes from, not to measure distances.

        Model lens: logistic regression vs LightGBM

        Both models re-rank the same 200 candidates for this user. Do they pick the same places, and for the same reasons? SHAP values explain each model's raw score, not the steered rank.

        Evaluation: is the learned ranker actually better?

        All test users, not just this one. Ranking quality (does a later-visited place reach the top 10?) and probability quality (is a predicted 1% chance really 1%?) are separate questions, so both are shown.
