# Restaurant detail (V8): rating histories

Generated by `src/29_export_restaurant_detail.py`. Reviews are the rankers' review set (clean NYC reviews, first review per user and restaurant); months are UTC months.

## What is exported

- 24 demo cases, 4,800 candidates, 3,005 different restaurants.
- Monthly star counts from January 2016 to April 2021 (64 months; charted from January 2017), plus one bucket for earlier reviews. Later months are left out because collection is incomplete.
- 2,195,239 reviews before the 2020-08-01 snapshot and 204,298 later reviews of these restaurants; 150,533 restaurant-months with at least one review.
- Files: 24 case files, 4.03 MB in total. They hold counts per month and star level only.

## Check against the rankers' snapshot

For all 4,800 candidates, the exported counts reproduce the case files: `reviews` and `recent_reviews` exactly, `rating` and `recent_rating` (smoothed toward the city-wide mean) within 1e-6.
City-wide mean before the snapshot: 4.1964 stars over 6,087,639 reviews (the rankers' prior, strength 10); standard deviation 1.1646.

## How much review evidence a restaurant has

- Reviews before the snapshot per restaurant: median 408, quartiles 161 to 909.
- Reviews in the 12 months before the snapshot: median 101; 14.3% of restaurants have fewer than 20, where the band is wide.
- Price level not listed: 15.4% of restaurants.

## Do ratings move?

Plain means, no smoothing. A move is a difference of at least 0.5 stars; both windows need at least 20 reviews.

- Before the snapshot: of 2,574 restaurants with 20+ reviews in the last 12 months, 22 (0.9%) have a 12-month mean that differs from their all-time mean.
- After the snapshot (August 2020 to April 2021, which the models could not see): of 1,975 restaurants with 20+ reviews in both periods, 113 (5.7%) moved from their 12-month mean at the snapshot (75 down, 38 up).
