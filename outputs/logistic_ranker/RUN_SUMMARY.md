All rows use the same 8,185 warm-catalog test users. The old 8,449-user baseline numbers used a different cold-start denominator and should not be compared directly. C=1 selected by validation; train-period snapshots create fitting examples, and validation examples join final fitting only after parameter selection. Training negatives are sampled unlabeled alternatives; test candidates are the full eligible catalog. Scores are not calibrated probabilities. Improvement is descriptive; statistical significance has not been claimed.

The final model uses 10 historical features: all-history and liked-history CF, log popularity, log recent popularity, smoothed historical and recent rating, rating change, recent share, months since last review, and a recent-rating/CF interaction. Coefficients are in coefficients.csv. No 2026 inspection fields or full-data metadata ratings are used as predictive features.

Published evaluation and provenance are in metrics.csv, run_metadata.json, training.log, and logistic_pipeline.joblib. Individual-user CSVs (test_per_user.csv and validation_per_user.csv) are kept local and regenerated by the training script. The report's baseline paragraph was filled from these real outputs after input-hash and sample-cohort checks. The compiled report retains the supplied formatting and one-page limit.
