# Restaurant detail (V8): theme summaries of review text

Generated by `src/30_theme_summaries.py` with `claude-sonnet-5-5` (effort medium). Reviews are the rankers' review set before the 2020-08-01 snapshot; texts shorter than 30 characters are left out.

## Coverage

- 3,005 restaurants in the demo cases: 2,804 summarised, 201 with fewer than 10 text reviews (no summary), 0 where no theme passed the checks, 0 not yet sent to the model.
- The model read 154,837 sampled reviews (up to 60 per restaurant, same mix of star ratings as the restaurant's text reviews), out of 843,909 text reviews of these restaurants.
- Tokens used: 13,122,361 input, 2,468,279 output.
- Files: 24 case files, 8.54 MB in total, with quotes.

## Checks on the model's answers

- Themes returned: 13,834; dropped because the label or summary names a person: 16; dropped because no quote passed: 13; published: 13,805.
- Quotes returned: 27,197. Not found in any sampled review (dropped): 31. Found in another review than the one cited (kept, citation corrected): 0. Too short or too long (dropped): 204. With a link, handle or phone number (dropped): 1. Naming a person by the script's cue patterns, which also catch some dish names (dropped): 157. Passed: 26,772.
- Review numbers outside the sample (ignored): 0.
- Not checked by the script: whether a summary sentence is a fair reading of the reviews, whether every tagged review really mentions the theme, and names of people that no cue pattern marks (the model is also told not to quote text that names a person).

- Themes per summarised restaurant: 4.9 on average.
- By tone: mixed 6,166 (their reviews average 3.81 stars); positive 4,980 (their reviews average 4.55 stars); negative 2,659 (their reviews average 2.59 stars).
